How Peer Evaluation Works

An automated, transparent four-step cycle that turns assignment reviews into deep learning experiences.

1

Enroll & Complete

Access rich video lessons, documents, notes, and track your progress in real-time.

2

Submit Project

Provide your project link, repo, and notes. The system auto-assigns an eligible peer reviewer.

3

Rubric Evaluation

Peers assess Content Quality, Clarity, Creativity, and Completeness with 1-5 star ratings.

4

Iterate & Excel

Receive actionable strengths and improvements. Reply to comments and watch your scores climb!
